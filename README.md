An offline web app that turns your photos into GTA 6-style cover art — the
multi-panel poster look with bold caption lettering and a Vice City sunset or
neon grade — and exports it as a PNG to share. Entry for
[make.fun's gta6-cover-art-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).

## What it does

- **Four layouts**: Nine-Panel Cover (the classic 3×3 with a centre title block),
  Hero + Three, Full Bleed, and Tilted Snapshots on an illustrated sunset backdrop.
- **Five grades**: Sunset, Neon Nights, Vice, Golden Hour, Noir — each a full
  colour treatment (sky, sun, photo wash, title gradient) applied to every panel.
- **Any photo**: load up to three photos from your machine, fill several panels
  with one photo or several. Click a panel to select it, drag to pan, scroll to
  zoom, or use the zoom slider. Photos are read locally and never uploaded.
- **Lettering**: a big Anton title with a gradient fill and dark outline, a neon
  Pacifico script subtitle, and a five-star badge ribbon. All editable.
- **Fast export**: one click exports a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or
  1920 × 1080.
- **The art is drawn in code**: every panel also has a procedural illustrated
  fallback scene (sunset sky, skyline, palms), plus halftone, grain and vignette —
  so the cover is complete even before a photo loads.

## Try it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network requests: `app/` is static files, and every font and
sample image it loads is a file under `app/`.

## Build the entry dossier

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `app/` and `screenshots/` to
the output directory and writes `dossier.json` with `buildDossier` from
`vendor/makefun-app.ts`.

## Originality

Everything in the app is original: the cover art is drawn by the app's own
canvas code, the sample photos are flat illustrations generated by
`tools/generate-samples.py`, and the lettering uses Anton and Pacifico, both
under the SIL Open Font License (texts in `app/fonts/`). No Rockstar Games
logo, artwork or font is used anywhere.
